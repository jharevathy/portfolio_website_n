## Where I started

My early work was radio frequency design: microstrip patches, impedance matching and long nights with a network analyser. It was precise, physical work, and AI felt like a different world.

## The first model I shipped

The turning point was a small internal tool that searched network incident reports. It was rough, but people used it every day, and that feedback loop hooked me.

> I didn't switch fields. I carried signal processing *into a new one.*

## What carried over

- Thinking in vectors and similarity long before I heard the word embedding.
- Respect for noise, and for measuring before optimising.
- Comfort with systems where small changes ripple a long way.

## If you are switching too

Pick one real problem from your current domain and solve it with AI. You will learn the tools, and you will bring something most AI engineers don't have: the context.
