## Why split at all

The monolith deployed as one unit, so a small change to billing meant redeploying search, carts and accounts. Splitting it let each team ship on its own schedule.

## Services talk through queues

Instead of calling each other directly, services publish events to RabbitMQ. The order service doesn't wait for email; it announces the order and moves on.

```javascript publish.js
const channel = await connection.createChannel();
await channel.assertQueue("order.created");
channel.sendToQueue("order.created", Buffer.from(JSON.stringify(order)));
```

> The queue is where services agree *to disagree.*

## What I would tell my past self

- Start with two or three services, not twelve.
- Add tracing on day one; debugging across services is hard without it.
- Make every message handler safe to run twice.
