HOW TO PUBLISH (this file itself is never shown on the website)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In Notepad++, copy this file, write your blog, and save it with a
   short name in lowercase with dashes, for example:  rag-chunking.txt
   That name becomes the link:  .../blog/post.html?p=rag-chunking
   (Encoding menu should say UTF-8, which is Notepad++'s default.)
2. On github.com open the repository, go to the folder  blog/posts
   and click  Add file  >  Upload files . Drag your .txt file in and click
   Commit changes .
3. Images: open the folder  blog/images , upload them the same way, and
   point to them in your text as  ![caption](images/file-name.png)
4. Wait about a minute and refresh the website. The newest Date is shown
   first on the homepage and in the library.

Header lines (only Title is really needed):
  Title      the headline
  Date       YYYY-MM-DD, used for the order (newest first)
  Topic      one word or two, used for the topic filter
  Tags       comma separated
  Summary    shown under the title and on the card
  Quote      the line shown on the Library card (default: your first > quote)
  Takeaway   one per line, shown in the "Key takeaways" box
  Cover      a number 1 to 6 for the gold cover pattern,
             or an image such as  images/cover.jpg
  Draft      yes  hides the blog until you change or delete this line
To edit a published blog, open its file on GitHub, click the pencil, change
it, and commit. To remove one, delete its file.
