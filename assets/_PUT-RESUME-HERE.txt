Put your résumé PDF in this folder and name it exactly:

    resume.pdf

so the full path is  assets/resume.pdf

The "Download résumé" buttons on the homepage (hero and contact) and on
every blog download it as "Nikhil-Salunke-Resume.pdf".
To update it later, upload a new resume.pdf here and GitHub replaces the old one.
Until this file exists, the buttons open the résumé on the old site instead.
